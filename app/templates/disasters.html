Disaster Information

🌊 Flood

Floods occur due to excessive rainfall, dam overflow and river flooding. Move to higher ground immediately.

🌍 Earthquake

Earthquakes are sudden movements of the Earth's crust. Drop, Cover and Hold during shaking.

🔥 Fire

Fire spreads rapidly through buildings. Use emergency exits and never use elevators.

🌪 Cyclone

Stay indoors, avoid coastal areas and keep emergency supplies ready.

☀ Heat Wave

Drink plenty of water, wear light clothing and avoid outdoor activities during peak hours.
